Beta Grappling Hook
==========================================================
Port of grappling hook from Beta 1.7.3 RopePlus mod.

Grappling Hook
----------------------------------------------------------
FishingRod-like item. Is crafted from strings and grapnel
head (crafted from iron). Grapnel head velocity is tuned
to upwards throwing: best for caving or traversing steep
hills. When grapnel head lands on valid spot the item
will disappear, grapnel head will turn into block and
will start lowering climbable rope. If any grappling
hook block is destroyed the entire thing breaks, dropping
grappling hook back it the position of the broken block.

Mod is in Beta
----------------------------------------------------------
Things might break and are a subject to change! Always
remember to make backups of your important data.
